Inputs: An Order object with item name, quantity, and unit price.
Output: A screen showing the item name, quantity, and total price.
Rules: Total comes from orderTotal(order); the screen must not calculate it itself.
Out of scope: Payments, editing the order, and saving data.
Check: Order of 2 x RM 12.50 shows a total of RM 25.00.
Navigation: A Back button uses Navigator.pop to return to the previous screen.
